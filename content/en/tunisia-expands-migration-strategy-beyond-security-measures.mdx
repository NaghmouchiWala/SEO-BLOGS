Tunisia's authorities announced on Tuesday that the country's fight against migrant trafficking networks will now incorporate humanitarian and development measures alongside security actions, said Mohamed Ali Nafti, a senior official, during a press briefing in Tunis.

## Expanded Scope of Migration Policy
Nafti emphasized that the Tunisian approach to migration is not limited to security operations. The government plans to strengthen cooperation with neighboring countries, improve border management, and simultaneously provide assistance to migrants in need. This includes facilitating access to basic services, legal aid, and pathways for regularisation where possible.

## Coordination with Regional Partners
The ministry highlighted ongoing talks with Libya, Algeria and Italy to disrupt trafficking routes. Joint patrols and intelligence sharing are set to increase, aiming to dismantle the networks that exploit vulnerable migrants. These efforts are intended to complement existing law‑enforcement actions while respecting international migration norms.

## Support Measures for Migrants
In addition to security, Tunisia will allocate resources to improve reception centres, health care and education for migrants arriving on its territory. The government also intends to launch vocational training programmes to aid integration and reduce the lure of smugglers. Nafti noted that these initiatives are part of a comprehensive strategy to address both the causes and consequences of irregular migration.

## Monitoring and Evaluation
The authorities plan to establish a monitoring body to assess the effectiveness of the combined security and humanitarian measures. Regular reports will be presented to the cabinet to adjust policies as needed.

The shift reflects a broader trend in Tunisian migration policy, seeking to balance border control with the protection of migrant rights. Observers expect further details on funding and implementation timelines in the coming weeks, as the government continues to refine its approach, reported by [mosaiquefm.net](https://www.mosaiquefm.net/fr/national-tunisie/1535856/la-tunisie-poursuit-la-lutte-contre-les-reseaux-de-trafic-de-migrants).
