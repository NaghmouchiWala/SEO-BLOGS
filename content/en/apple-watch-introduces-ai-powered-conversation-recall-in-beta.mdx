Apple has begun beta testing a set of Audio Intelligence features for the Apple Watch that aim to make everyday conversations easier to manage. The new capabilities—dubbed "rewind" and "recap"—use on‑device machine learning to capture spoken content, generate a short summary, and let users replay specific moments later. This marks the first time the watch can act as a personal note‑taker for verbal exchanges, a function that could reshape how professionals and casual users interact with their wearable.

## How the features work
The "rewind" function records a segment of a conversation and stores it securely on the device. When the user activates the feature, the watch presents a timeline that can be scrubbed forward or backward, allowing a quick replay of missed or unclear statements. The "recap" mode goes a step further by processing the captured audio and producing a concise textual summary that appears on the watch face or in the companion iPhone app. Both tools rely on Apple's on‑device AI, meaning that the raw audio never leaves the user’s hardware, preserving privacy while delivering real‑time assistance.
